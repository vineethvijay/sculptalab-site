No two bodies are the same, so no two plans should be. SculptaLab learns yours, scores every muscle, and builds you up naturally, week by week, into your strongest, best-looking shape.

How it works

  1. 01

    Your day, decided.

    The Today card knows your plan and your usual workout time. Training day? One tap to start. Rest day? It quietly shows what’s next.

  2. 02

    A plan made for your body.

    Answer three questions and get a week that fits you: 3–4 days built on legs and glutes, with back, shoulders and arms mixed in, and cardio that earns points. It adapts as you progress.

  3. 03

    Guided, set by set.

    Your session runs itself: last time’s weights, rest timers, one-tap logging and quick swaps. Prop your phone up and pose tracking counts clean reps and scores your range of motion.

  4. 04

    Watch your foundation grow.

    Each set lands on your muscle map. See which muscles hit their weekly target, which are lagging, and how your Sculpta score moves week to week.
