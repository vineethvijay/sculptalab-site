Most apps count reps. SculptaLab watches every one — the depth, the lockout, the squeeze at the top — and shapes your training around what your body actually did.

How it works

  1. 01

    Your day, decided.

    The Today card knows your plan and your usual workout time. Training day? One tap to start. Rest day? It quietly shows what’s next.

  2. 02

    A coach in your camera.

    Prop your phone up and train. On-device pose tracking follows your hips and knees, counts clean reps and flags the ones that didn’t count.

  3. 03

    Plans that build you.

    Structured weekly plans around the moves that matter for glutes — with sets, reps and rest laid out so you just show up.

  4. 04

    See yourself change.

    Every session lands in your stats: volume, streaks and rep quality over time, so progress is something you see, not guess.
